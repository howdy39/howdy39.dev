description: "Is it bad to be motivated by gratitude at work? I don't think so. But if gratitude becomes the goal itself, you start depending on other people's reactions, and on days with few reactions you can lose sight of why you work. Using help desk work in corporate IT as an example, I wrote about how fewer inquiries thanks to automation and better documentation is also the result of good work, and how there is gratitude you never see."
