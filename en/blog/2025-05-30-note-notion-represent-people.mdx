description: "I compared three ways to represent a person on a Notion page: emoji, a page, or a mention. Emoji are limited to 500 per workspace and Notion AI can't read them. Mentions don't show an icon and trigger a notification. I recommend a self-introduction database with a page for each person, which is clear to both people and AI and has no limit on icons. I'd use mentions only when a notification is needed."
