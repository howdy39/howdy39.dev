title: "I no longer immediately say 'let's hire more' when told 'we're short-staffed'"
description: "When someone told me 'we're short-staffed,' a few years ago I'd split about 50/50 between 'let's hire more' and 'let's automate and review the work so we can manage with the current team.' Now that AI is an option, I say 'let's hire more' less often. Adding people casually may be easy in the short term but might not help the team in the long run. If you'll work another 20 to 30 years, AI skills are a must, and that's what I wrote about."
