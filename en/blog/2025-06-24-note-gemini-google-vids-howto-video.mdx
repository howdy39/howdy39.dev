title: "Creating how-to videos by combining Gemini and Google Vids"
description: "Adding captions to how-to videos used to take too much effort to be realistic. When the Gemini app gained video upload, I wondered whether that would change, so I tried it. Record your screen, have Gemini create a subtitle file (SRT), then add closed captions in Google Vids. Since Vids can't export with captions, either record the captioned playback or embed the subtitles with ffmpeg."
