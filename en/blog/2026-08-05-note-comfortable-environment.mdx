title: "Is it really good to keep working in a well-organized environment?"
description: "Is it really good to keep working in a well-organized environment, with a solid budget, supportive executives and talented teammates? Early in a career, I think it is valuable because you learn what the ideal looks like. But some skills are hard to build there: winning budget, persuading executives, and setting priorities with limited resources. I wrote about why you need both, experience in an ideal environment and experience moving a real one that isn't organized yet."
