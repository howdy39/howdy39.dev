description: "Back offices have no market competition to be chosen. They decide things like return-to-office policy, expense reports and PC specs, and employees have no choice, so the experience won't improve unless we make it a priority. A bad experience also leads to shadow IT and shadow AI, which is a security risk. I propose involving real users when choosing tools and measuring satisfaction regularly."
