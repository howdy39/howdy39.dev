description: "I wrote about how raising your interpretation skills changes the world you see. In 2023, I used 'sorry' 42 times and 'thank you' 385 times on Slack, a ratio of about 1 to 18. When someone points out a gap in a document, I reply 'thank you for noticing,' not 'sorry,' and add what I fixed. That makes it easier for them to speak up next time. The facts don't change, but when you change how you interpret them, the world slowly changes."
