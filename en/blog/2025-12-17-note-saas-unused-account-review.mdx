title: "Four points for effectively reviewing unused SaaS accounts"
description: "I wrote four points for reviewing unused SaaS accounts effectively. Deleting accounts of people who left can often be automated with SCIM, but accounts of current employees who just don't use a tool are hard to automate. The points are: start with the SaaS that are easiest to review, use an opt-out approach (delete accounts that don't respond) when usage dates are available and no data depends on the account, build something between manual and automatic with APIs and spreadsheets, and use a SaaS management tool."
