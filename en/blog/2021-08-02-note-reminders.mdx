title: "The one who reminds and the one who is reminded"
description: "I saw someone who used to be on the receiving end of reminders start reminding other people, and I wrote about what that made me think. A reminder comes from not trusting 100% that something is done, and the person being reminded shouldn't forget they are using up the other person's mental resources. I think when the one who is reminded becomes the one who reminds, their level of work has gone up."
