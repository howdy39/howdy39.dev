title: "How to safely share Google Workspace's NotebookLM with external users"
description: "I wrote about safely sharing Google Workspace's NotebookLM with partners outside your organization. In the Workspace edition, public sharing is disabled for security reasons, so you can't share with accounts outside the organization. The workaround is to go through a Google Group: create a group for the external partners, set it as the NotebookLM share target, and allow external members in the group settings. Note that on October 14, 2025, a change on Google's side made this procedure unusable."
