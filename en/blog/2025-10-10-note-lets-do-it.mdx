title: "When unsure about something new, default to 'let's do it'"
description: "When I'm unsure about something new, I default to 'let's do it.' It started with Masayoshi Son using the phrase on Twitter around 2010, and experiencing that culture around 2012 while supporting system development for the SoftBank Group. Since becoming a manager, I've used it with my team too: if the goal is likely to be met, I leave the method to them, and we always hold a retrospective. And when I'm unsure about something we already do, my default is 'let's stop.'"
