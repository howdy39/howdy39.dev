title: "Design guidelines for disabling export in Notion in a business environment"
description: "Notion's export feature is handy, but it carries the risk of data being taken out. Only the Enterprise plan can disable export, so balancing security and backups becomes the challenge. I compared four patterns of workspace and teamspace settings. My recommendation is pattern C: disable export at the workspace level, override it by enabling it in teamspaces, limit teamspace creation to workspace owners, and keep a backup teamspace for admins."
