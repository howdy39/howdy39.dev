title: "Creating a Nuxt middleware that blocks direct access"
description: "In an SPA where initial setup happens on a different page, you may want to block direct access to deep pages, so I show how to build a Nuxt middleware for that. On a direct visit it redirects to the page that does the initial setup. The mechanism is simple: it treats a visit as direct access when from.name and route.name match. I published demo pages and the full code."
