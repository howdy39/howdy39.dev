title: "Rationality and diversity, seen through company email addresses"
description: "At my current company, new hires can freely choose the local part of their work email address (the part before the @) during onboarding. Some software engineers care more about their ID than their name, and since many services make it hard to change an email address, an ID can be steadier than a surname that may change at marriage. What matters most, I think, is whether your choice reflects your own intention."
